RECRUIT DIVISION

> Shadowboxing
Stand with one foot slightly in front of the other. Keep your hands up by your face. Throw your hands out one at a time, and twist your body when you punch to add power. Keep moving your feet.
WATCH HOW TO DO IT
> Heavy Bag Work
Imagine the bag is a person. Hit it with your knuckles, not your palms. Every time you throw a punch, make sure your other hand stays up by your chin to "block" hits.
WATCH HOW TO DO IT
> Footwork
Practice moving around. If you want to move forward, step with your front foot first. If you want to move backward, step with your back foot first. Never let your feet cross each other or you will lose your balance.
