> Shadowboxing Routine
Objective: Perfect your punching form.

> Heavy Bag / Pads
Objective: Build impact and conditioning.
Steps: Strike with your knuckles. Keep your opposite hand glued to your chin at all times to block counters. Snap your punch back to your face immediately after contact.
> Footwork
Objective: Master movement and balance.
Steps: Moving forward? Step with the front foot first. Moving back? Step with the back foot first. Stay light on your toes and keep your weight centered.
